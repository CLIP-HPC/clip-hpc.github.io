# Trainings

Computational biology, biostatistics, and HPC courses for CLIP researchers.

The Vienna BioCenter training program runs regular computational biology,
biostatistics, and high-performance computing (HPC) courses. If you are new to
HPC or to SLURM, take a course before you start on CBE.

- The [VBCF training schedule](https://training.vbcf.ac.at/training/schedule.php)
  lists all upcoming courses.
- The [VBCF HPC training track](https://training.vbcf.ac.at/training/hpc.php)
  covers cluster fundamentals and container technologies.

To ask about a course or to suggest a topic, email
[contact@clip.science](mailto:contact@clip.science) with your affiliation and
the topic you are interested in.
