Trainings

Computational biology, biostatistics, and HPC courses for CLIP researchers.

The Vienna BioCenter training program runs regular computational biology, biostatistics, and high-performance computing (HPC) courses. If you are new to HPC or to SLURM, take a course before you start on CBE.

  • The VBCF training schedule lists all upcoming courses.
  • The VBCF HPC training track covers cluster fundamentals and container technologies.

To ask about a course or to suggest a topic, email contact@clip.science with your affiliation and the topic you are interested in.

Get help

Account holders can open a ticket in the HPC Servicedesk. Prospective users can email contact@clip.science. See Support for all options.
