# Careers

Open positions on the CLIP infrastructure team.

CLIP's IT infrastructure team is actively recruiting for technical positions.

## Open position: Linux performance engineering &mdash; HPC systems engineer

**Responsibilities:** Design, implementation, and documentation of heterogeneous IT
infrastructure in a scientific environment, including high-performance computing
clusters, storage systems, and network infrastructure, in collaboration with the
wider IT team.

**Qualifications:** Strong interest in infrastructure work, practical experience
with Linux/Unix system administration and troubleshooting, self-motivation, and
proficiency in English (the team's working language).

**Benefits:** Flexible tool selection, educational support, flexible working hours,
autonomy in project work, on-site dining, childcare services, and public transit
subsidies.

**Compensation:** Unlimited employment contract with a gross annual salary starting
at €56,000, negotiable based on qualifications and experience.

**How to apply:** Submit a résumé, motivation letter, and relevant certificates via
[contact@clip.science](mailto:contact@clip.science).
