# About

Founding partners, mission, and citation guidelines for CLIP.

The Cloud Infrastructure Platform (CLIP) offers an interdisciplinary, low entry
barrier approach to high-performance computing. It enables researchers, wherever
their lab is located, to access powerful compute resources for methods such as
DNA sequencing, theoretical modeling, and molecular visualization.

## Founding partners

- [Gregor Mendel Institute (GMI)](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/gmi)
- [Marietta Blau Institute (MBI)](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/mbi/), formed by
  combining the Institute of High Energy Physics (HEPHY) and the Stefan
  Meyer Institute (SMI)
- [Institute of Molecular Biotechnology (IMBA)](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/imba/home)
- [Research Institute of Molecular Pathology (IMP)](https://www.imp.ac.at/)

All institutes except IMP are part of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW).
CLIP is open to researchers at all ÖAW institutes, not only the founding
partners, and at IMP. See [Access](/access/#who-can-use-clip)
for details.

## Cite CLIP {#cite}

If CLIP resources contributed to a publication or other research output,
acknowledge them with the following text:

> The authors acknowledge the CLIP cluster for the computational
> results that were presented.

If other Vienna BioCenter facilities also contributed, the
[VBC acknowledgement tool](https://acknowledgement.vbc.ac.at/) combines their
wording into one statement; select **Scientific Computing - CLIP** there. The
documentation also explains how to
[acknowledge CLIP](https://docs.vbc.ac.at/research-computing/acknowledgements/).
