About

Founding partners, mission, and citation guidelines for CLIP.

The Cloud Infrastructure Platform (CLIP) offers an interdisciplinary, low entry barrier approach to high-performance computing. It enables researchers, wherever their lab is located, to access powerful compute resources for methods such as DNA sequencing, theoretical modeling, and molecular visualization.

Founding partners

  • Gregor Mendel Institute (GMI)
  • Marietta Blau Institute (MBI), formed by combining the Institute of High Energy Physics (HEPHY) and the Stefan Meyer Institute (SMI)
  • Institute of Molecular Biotechnology (IMBA)
  • Research Institute of Molecular Pathology (IMP)

All institutes except IMP are part of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW). CLIP is open to researchers at all ÖAW institutes, not only the founding partners, and at IMP. See Access for details.

Cite CLIP

If CLIP resources contributed to a publication or other research output, acknowledge them with the following text:

The authors acknowledge the CLIP cluster for the computational results that were presented.

If other Vienna BioCenter facilities also contributed, the VBC acknowledgement tool combines their wording into one statement; select Scientific Computing - CLIP there. The documentation also explains how to acknowledge CLIP.
